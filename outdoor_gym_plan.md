# DIY Outdoor Gym — Complete Building Plan

> **Note:** The Facebook video link requires login to view, so this plan is based on proven DIY outdoor gym designs from multiple verified sources (eathealthy365.com, ihomegarden.com, fitstream.com, outdoorfit.online). The build below follows the most popular and safe DIY approach: a combined pull-up bar + squat rack station with dip bars and a plyo box.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

**Station:** Combined Pull-Up / Squat Rack + Dip Station + Plyometric Box
**Footprint:** ~6 ft wide x 4 ft deep (plus ~2 ft clearance on all sides for safe use)
**Estimated Build Time:** 2–3 weekends
**Estimated Total Cost:** $550 – $900 (materials only; excludes heavy equipment like plates/barbell)
**Lifespan:** 10–15 years with proper sealant maintenance

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP BUILD INSTRUCTIONS

### PHASE 1: Prepare the Site & Cut All Wood (Day 1)
1. **Clear and level** a 10 ft × 10 ft area. Remove grass, rocks, and roots.
2. **Lay out the rectangle:** Mark a 4 ft (front-to-back) × 6 ft (side-to-side) rectangle with spray paint or string. This is your post layout.
3. **Cut all lumber** per the cut list above. Sand rough edges.
4. **Apply wood preservative** to ALL cut ends and the bottom 6 inches of each post (the part that goes into concrete). Let dry.

### PHASE 2: Dig Footings & Set Posts (Day 1–2)
1. **Dig 4 holes** at the corners of your rectangle:
   - Diameter: 12 inches
   - Depth: 36 inches (3 ft) — below frost line in most climates
   - Add 2 inches of gravel at the bottom of each hole for drainage
2. **Place your posts** (A, B, C, D) into the holes.
3. **Check plumb:** Use your 4-foot level on two adjacent sides of each post. Adjust until perfectly vertical.
4. **Brace posts temporarily** with scrap wood stakes and level cross-braces.
5. **Mix and pour concrete:**
   - Follow bag instructions (usually ~3 bags per hole for 60 lb mix)
   - Add water gradually — mix should be like thick peanut butter
   - Poke the wet concrete with a stick to release air bubbles
   - Shape the top in a slight dome so water runs off
6. **Let cure:** Wait 48–72 hours before touching the frame. Do not rush this.

### PHASE 3: Attach Crossbeams (Day 3)
1. **Position crossbeam E (top)** at 8 ft 6 in above ground level — attach between front posts A and B.
2. **Position crossbeam F (mid)** at 5 ft 0 in — attach between front and rear posts for squat rack support.
3. **Position crossbeam G (lower)** at 3 ft 0 in — for storage of accessories.
4. **Pre-drill** ⅜" pilot holes through each crossbeam into the posts (2 holes per connection).
5. **Drive ½" × 8" lag bolts** with washers at each pre-drilled hole. Use the impact driver.
6. **Double-check everything is square** (measure diagonals — they should be equal).

### PHASE 4: Install Pull-Up Bar (Day 3)
1. **Measure the exact distance** between the inside faces of posts A and B at the pull-up bar height.
2. **Cut the 1¼" steel pipe** to that exact measurement (the pipe should span the inside gap — not the full width of the posts).
   - If posts are 6 ft apart center-to-center with 3.5" actual width: subtract 7" from 72" = **65" pipe length**
3. **Drill 1¼" holes** through both posts where you want the bar (use a Forstner or spade bit). Holes should be centered in the post width and aligned perfectly.
4. **Insert the pipe** through the holes from outside to inside.
5. **Secure** with ⅜" lock washers and nuts on each end, or weld stop collars (recommended).

### PHASE 5: Build Dip Station (Day 4)
1. **Position dip posts (H, I)** approximately 3–4 ft in front of the main structure, or attached to the rear side.
2. **Mount the dip grip beam (J)** between posts H and I at about waist height (30 inches from ground).
3. **Alternative:** Build freestanding dip bars by setting posts H and I into concrete blocks (no dug footings needed — use 4×4 post bases with gravel fill for a cheaper option).

### PHASE 6: Build Plyo Box (Day 4)
1. **Lay out side panels** using parts K (4 pcs) and L (4 pcs) — create two identical 20" × 16" rectangular frames.
2. **Lay out front panels** using parts M (4 pcs) and N (4 pcs) — create two identical 20" × 20" frames.
3. **Add cross-braces (O)** inside each panel for rigidity.
4. **Attach plywood faces (P, Q)** to the front and back of each frame using deck screws.
5. **Stack the three boxes** in order of height:
   - Box 1 (tallest): 20" high — for box jumps
   - Box 2 (medium): 16" high — for step-ups
   - Box 3 (shortest): 12" high — for push-up elevation

### PHASE 7: Final Touches (Day 5)
1. **Apply deck stain/sealant** to all exposed wood. Use a brush or roller. Two coats recommended.
2. **Lay down rubber mats** (4×6 stall mats) under the main structure for joint protection and equipment safety.
3. **Inspect all bolts** — re-tighten any that feel loose.
4. **Test the structure:** Hang from the pull-up bar (200+ lb person), do a few squats on the rack. It should be solid with zero wobble.

## 7. SAFETY CHECKLIST

- [ ] Concrete fully cured 72 hours before loading structure
- [ ] All lag bolts torqued and backed up with nuts
- [ ] Pull-up bar secured with lock washers or welded collars
- [ ] Posts are plumb on two sides — verified with level
- [ ] Diagonal measurements of frame are equal (square)
- [ ] Rubber mats laid under all drop zones
- [ ] Clearance of 2+ feet on all sides of equipment
- [ ] Overhead clearance of 10+ feet (no trees/wires)
- [ ] All cut ends of lumber treated with preservative
- [ ] Hardware is hot-dip galvanized or stainless (NO regular steel)
