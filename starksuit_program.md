# LeoMoves-Inspired Return-to-Flow Program
**Built for Vasko — "Project Starksuit"**
*Phase 1: Foundation & Reawakening (Weeks 1–4)*

---

## PHILOSOPHY

This program follows LeoMoves principles: bodyweight-first movement, animal flow / locomotion patterns, mobility woven into every session, and "flow sequences" instead of traditional sets/reps. No rigid rep counting — instead, you'll move through patterns for reps or time. The emphasis is on quality of movement, joint health, and building the neural pathways that make you a better mover.

**Key safety notes for your body:**
- **Left hip flexor history:** We avoid deep end-range hip flexion under load. Movements are controlled, no explosive hip-hinging.
- **Neck/trap history:** Floor work uses neutral spine cues. No heavy loaded carries until Phase 3.
- **Baby-bend ergonomics:** We'll build hip-hinge mobility and posterior chain strength to counteract the daily bending.

## STRUCTURE

- **3 days/week** (e.g., Mon / Wed / Fri — at minimum 1 rest day between sessions)
- **Each session: 20–25 minutes**
- **Daily micro-mobility:** 3–5 min of the warm-up sequence on off-days (picks up baby easier)

### Weekly Layout

| Monday | Wednesday | Friday |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Session A | Session B | Session C |
| (Flow + Mobility) | (Flow + Pull/Push) | (Flow + Core/Locomotion) |

---

## SESSION A: Flow & Mobility (Monday)

**Warm-up (3–4 min)** — Do every day, off-days too
1. Cat-Cow x10 (slow, breath-synced)
2. Thread-the-needle each side x6
3. World's Greatest Stretch x4 per side
4. Wrist circles x10 each direction (prep for floor weight-bearing)

**Main Flow — 3 rounds, 60 sec rest between**
1. **Sidemonkey to Standup** — 6 reps per side
   - The foundational LeoMoves move. Start position: one hand floor, one leg out wide, other leg bent. Explode up to stand without using the floor hand if possible. Builds hip mobility, lateral strength, and coordination.
2. **Shuffle Step** — 10 steps per direction
   - Low squat position, shuffle side to side. Great for hip internal/external rotation and coordination.
3. **Swipe Step** — 8 reps per side
   - Cross-body stepping pattern. Opens the posterior chain and challenges balance.
4. **Bear Crawl** — 20 seconds forward, 20 seconds backward
   - All fours, knees hovering 1 inch off ground. Builds shoulder stability and core anti-extension.
5. **Crab Walk** — 20 seconds forward, 20 seconds backward
   - Hips elevated, opposite hand-foot reach. Opens tight hips and shoulders simultaneously.
6. **Dead Bug** — 8 reps per side (slow)
   - Lie on back, press lower back into floor. Core stability without neck strain.

**Cooldown (2 min)**
- Child's pose (30 sec)
- Seated forward fold (30 sec)
- Neck releases — gentle ear-to-shoulder each side (30 sec each, *no force*)
- Deep breathing (60 sec)

---

## SESSION B: Flow + Pull/Push (Wednesday)

**Warm-up (3–4 min)**
1. Cat-Cow x10
2. Thoracic rotations on side (8 per side)
3. Band pull-aparts → use resistance band or just arm circles (10 each direction)
4. Wrist prep (prayer stretch, reverse prayer, each 20 sec)

**Main Flow — 3 rounds, 60 sec rest between**
1. **Sidemonkey to Standup** — 6 reps per side
2. **Reach Back** — 8 reps per side
   - From a low position, reach one arm back under the body while shifting weight. Opens chest, shoulders, and hip flexors — targeted therapy for tight shoulders.
3. **Push-up flow** — 8–10 reps (your baseline)
   - Standard pushup, *slow* eccentric (3 sec down). If form breaks, drop to knees. Quality > quantity.
4. **Assisted Pull-up (power tower)** — 5–8 reps
   - Use the assisted dip/pull-up machine on your power tower. Set weight so the last 2 reps are controlled and hard. Focus on scapular depression — no shrugging (protects traps).
5. **Dips (power tower)** — 5–8 reps
   - Shallow range (60°), no deep stretch. Keep shoulders down and away from ears.
6. **Side Kick Crunch** — 8 reps per side
   - Lying on side, kick top leg up while crunching torso. Builds obliques and hip stability.

**Cooldown (2 min)**
- Chest doorway stretch (30 sec per side)
- Child's pose (30 sec)
- Wrist stretches (30 sec each direction)
- Deep breathing (60 sec)

---

## SESSION C: Flow + Core & Locomotion (Friday)

**Warm-up (3–4 min)**
1. Cat-Cow x10
2. Hip circles (standing, large) x8 each direction
3. Inverted arm circles (bent over, small arms) x10 each direction
4. Ankle rocks (knee-to-wall) x6 per side

**Main Flow — 3 rounds, 60 sec rest between**
1. **Kneeling Cross Kick to Squat** — 8 reps per side
   - From kneeling, kick one leg across and under, drive to stand. Excellent for hip flexor *mobility* (not strain) and posterior chain activation.
2. **Scorpion Reach** — 6 reps per side
   - From prone face-down, reach one arm back toward opposite foot. Thoracic mobility + glute activation.
3. **Beast Hold** — 30 seconds
   - All fours, knees hover 1 inch. Hold perfectly still. Builds deep core and shoulder stability. Progress to 30 sec holds.
4. **Slow Squat to Cossack** — 10 reps per side
   - Down to squat, shift to one side (Cossack stretch), return. Opens adductors, builds single-leg stability.
5. **Plank to Down Dog** — 8 reps (alternating sides)
   - From plank, push hips up to down dog, then back. Shoulder mobility + hamstring + core.
6. **Glute Bridge Hold** — 30 seconds
   - Lie on back, drive hips up. Squeeze glutes at top. Counteracts sitting + baby-bending. Builds posterior chain.

**Cooldown (2 min)**
- Figure-4 stretch (each side, 30 sec)
- Happy baby pose (30 sec)
- Neck releases (30 sec each side, *gentle only*)
- Deep breathing (60 sec)

## OFF-DAY MICRO-MOBILITY (3–5 min, do on rest days)

Pick 4 of these when picking up baby:
- Hip flexor stretch (half-kneeling, 30 sec/side) — *stop if left side catches*
- Seated cat-cow (sitting tall, arch/round spine, 8 reps)
- Shoulder dislocates (use a broomstick or resistance band, 8 slow reps)
- Thoracic extension over foam roller or rolled towel (1 min)
- Child's pose with side walk (30 sec)

## PROGRESSION RULES

**You progress when you can complete ALL of the following for a full week:**
1. Every flow element with clean technique
2. The last rep of every set feels hard but controllable
3. You feel recovered (not sore or fatigued) by the next session

**Progression path (Phase 1 → Phase 2):**
- Add a 4th round to each flow
- Increase bear/crab crawl to 30 sec
- Drop to fewer assisted pull-up lbs (heavier assist = lighter weight)
- Begin adding 10 lb dumbbell carries (Phase 2)

**Phase 1 target: Week 4 should feel manageable, not brutal.** You should feel MORE energized after sessions, not drained. Energy is the metric.

## FORM REMINDERS

- **Neck:** Gaze slightly forward on all floor work. Never craned back looking at ceiling.
- **Hip flexors:** If you feel pinching in the front of your hip, STOP and reduce range.
- **Shoulders:** Always "pack" them — draw them down and back before push/pull movements.
- **Breathing:** Exhale on exertion, inhale on the return. Never hold your breath.
- **Baby consideration:** Do sessions *before* baby nap windows if possible, not right after feeding.
