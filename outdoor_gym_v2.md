# DIY Backyard Calisthenics Fortress — Complete Building Plan

> **Based on reference images from @calistenics.withkaylee's Instagram build.** This is a complete outdoor playground/gym with upper level netting, shade sail, monkey bars, rope tower, swings, dip bars, pull-up bar, TRX rings, and more.

---

## 1. STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

**Design:** Two-zone wooden adventure structure
- **Zone A — Main Adventure Tower** (~12 ft x 12 ft footprint): 14 ft tall, upper level with safety netting and shade sail
- **Zone B — Calisthenics Frame** (~10 ft x 5 ft footprint): 12 ft tall, monkey bars + pull-up + dips
- **Total Footprint:** ~22 ft x 12 ft minimum (allow 5+ ft clearance on all sides)

**Features:**
- Pull-up bar (chin-ups, toe-to-bar, ball slams)
- Monkey bar ladder (8–12 rungs)
- Dip station at 2 heights
- Rope tower (climbing rope + hanging rope obstacles)
- TRX/gymnastic rings
- Disc/swing seats (2–3 swings)
- Hammock attachment point
- Upper level with safety netting on all sides
- Black shade sail canopy on top
- Wood climbing holds on posts
- Bungee/abduct bar for core work

**Estimated Build Time:** 4–6 weekends
**Estimated Total Cost:** $2,200 – $3,500 (materials only)

## 6. STEP-BY-STEP BUILD INSTRUCTIONS

### PHASE 1: Layout & Foundation (Weekend 1)

**Step 1 — Mark the footprint:**
- Lay out Zone A (12×12 ft rectangle) and Zone B (10×5 ft rectangle) connected side-by-side
- Use spray paint or string line. Add 5 ft clearance on all sides
- **CALL 811** before digging — mark underground utilities
- Mark post locations for all 12 main posts (4 corners of Zone A, 4 corners of Zone B, 4 upper-level posts above Zone A)

**Step 2 — Dig all footings:**
- Diameter: 12–14 inches
- Depth: 36 inches (minimum — go 48 inches if in frost-prone area)
- Add 2 inches of gravel at bottom of each hole for drainage
- For Zone B posts (lighter structure): 30 inches deep is acceptable

**Step 3 — Set 6×6 posts (Zone A — A1–B4):**
- Place posts into holes, check plumb with level on two sides
- Brace each post with temporary 2×4 stakes
- Ensure posts are square (measure diagonals — must be equal)
- Mix and pour fast-setting concrete (2–3 bags per hole)
- Poke concrete with stick to release air bubbles
- Slope top of concrete slightly away from post for drainage
- **Cure 48–72 hours before proceeding**

**Step 4 — Set 4×4 posts (Zone B — F1–F4):**
- Same process as above, 30 inches deep
- Align top of Zone B frame to desired height (~12 ft finished)

### PHASE 2: Main Frame Assembly (Weekend 2)

**Step 5 — Install mid-level beams (D9–D12 at ~7 ft):**
- Cut 6×6 beams to 10 ft 6 in (cross-brace beams)
- Attach between adjacent posts using ½" × 8" lag bolts (2 per connection)
- These provide structural rigidity before the top beams

**Step 6 — Install main span beams (D1–D4 at ~10 ft):**
- Position front beam (D1) between A1–A4 at 10 ft height
- Position rear beam (D2) between B1–B4 at 10 ft height
- Position side beams (D3, D4) connecting A1–B1 and A4–B4
- Pre-drill ¼" pilot holes through beams into posts
- Use ½" × 8" lag bolts with washers — 3 per connection point
- **Use post base connectors (Simpson GB series)** between beam and post for added strength — bolt through pre-drilled holes

**Step 7 — Install Zone B beams (E1–E3):**
- E1 (top): 10 ft beam at 12 ft height between F1–F2
- E2 (top): 10 ft beam at 12 ft height between F3–F4
- E3 (mid — pull-up bar): 10 ft beam at ~9 ft height between F1–F4

**Step 8 — Add cross-bracing:**
- Install diagonal 2×4 braces from lower posts to mid-level beams on both zones
- Angle: 45° preferred. Use speed square to mark
- Secure with 3" deck screws (2 per brace) + ½" lag bolts at beam connections

### PHASE 3: Upper Level & Monkey Bars (Weekend 3)

**Step 9 — Attach upper-level posts (C1–C4):**
- Bolt 12 ft posts on top of A1–A4 posts using steel post caps or heavy-duty bracket plates
- Use ¾" G4 hex bolts (3 per post) through metal connector plates
- Verify all four upper posts are level and square

**Step 10 — Install upper level beams (D5–D8):**
- Build a 12×12 ft ring at ~13 ft height using 6×6 beams
- Connect with metal post caps or butt joints + lag bolts
- This is the top ring where netting and shade sail attach

**Step 11 — Install upper deck joists (J1–J4):**
- Place 2×6 joists between D5–D8 at 16" OC spacing
- Use joist hangers (Simpson JH series, ~$2/each) — buy 10–12
- Secure with ½" × 3" nails or structural screws

**Step 12 — Install upper deck planks (M1–M4):**
- Lay 2×6 deck boards across joists
- Leave ¼" gap between boards for drainage
- Secure with 3" deck screws (2 per joist per board)

**Step 13 — Install monkey bar frame:**
- Attach 8 metal monkey bar rungs (16–21.5") across the top beam E1/E2
- Use monkey bar mounting plates — bolt through pre-drilled holes in beam
- Rung spacing: 12–14 inches apart (standard monkey bar spacing)
- Rungs should stick out ~6 inches on each side for grip
- **Test every rung** before continuing — they bear full body weight

**Step 14 — Install pull-up bar (N1):**
- Center 1¼" steel pipe between Zone B mid-beam (E3)
- Mount using metal beam clamps (U-bolts) or drill through beam and secure with nuts on both sides
- The bar should be at ~9 ft height for chin-ups and toe-to-bar

### PHASE 4: Dip Bars, Rope Tower, & Swings (Weekend 4)

**Step 15 — Build dip station:**
- Low dip bars (G1–G2 at 8 ft): Mount 3 ft steel pipes between posts using metal beam clamps
- High dip bars (G3–G4 at 7 ft): Similar mount on rear dip posts
- Dip bar height: 28–32 inches from ground for comfortable use
- Secure with ⅜" × 6" lag bolts through beam into posts

**Step 16 — Install rope tower:**
- Drill ¾" hole through top beam at desired rope position
- Thread ¾" or 1" fibershed climbing rope through (100 ft roll)
- Secure top with rope thimble + carabiner or wrap + rope clips
- Hang 6 rope course discs (every 3–4 ft) using rope clips or drilled holes
- Bottom of rope: cut to ~2 ft above ground for easy access

**Step 17 — Install TRX / gymnastic ring attachment:**
- Mount heavy-duty eye bolts into top beam of Zone A
- Attach TRX suspension straps or wooden gymnastic rings
- Ring height: adjustable at ~9–10 ft for ring dips and muscle-ups
- Use swivel carabiners to prevent strap twisting

**Step 18 — Install swings:**
- Mount swing chains (7 ft) with S-hooks to upper beams
- Attach 2 disc/tire swings (36" or 42" diameter)
- Position swings at ~16 ft clearance from ground to seat
- Add 6–8 ft spacing between swing seats for safety

**Step 19 — Install hammock:**
- Attach 6 ft support posts (H1–H2) or use existing frame
- Install ⅜" × 4" stainless eye bolts at opposite ends
- Hang hammock with chain or rope — target seat height 18 inches from ground

**Step 20 — Add bungee / abduct bar:**
- Run ¾" bungee cord between two posts at ~4 ft height
- Attach to ¼" steel cable or rope for horizontal core exercises

### PHASE 5: Netting, Shade, & Finishing (Weekend 5)

**Step 21 — Install safety netting:**
- Attach black safety netting (10 ft × 200 ft rolls) around all open sides of the upper level
- Use zip ties (UV-rated, black) or rope — attach every 6–8 inches along the top and side beams
- Netting should cover the full 12×12 ft perimeter up to the top ring
- Cut netting to fit using utility knife
- Reinforce corners with wood cleats (see image reference — rounded wooden corner patches)

**Step 22 — Install shade sails:**
- Mount stainless steel eye bolts at each corner of the upper level top ring
- Attach two 14×14×14 ft black shade sails in a criss-cross pattern (X shape)
- Use turnbuckles for tensioning — pull taut but leave slight sag for water runoff
- Shade sails should overlap in center for full coverage

**Step 23 — Install wood climbing holds:**
- Bolt wooden climbing holds directly into side of 6×6 posts at various heights
- Space 12–18 inches apart for a climbing path up the posts
- Use ⅜" stainless lag bolts — drill ¼" pilot holes first

**Step 24 — Paint / stain:**
- Apply exterior wood stain/sealant to ALL exposed wood surfaces
- Two coats recommended, following grain direction
- Let first coat dry 24 hours before second
- Paint all steel pipes and hardware with red oxide primer + exterior paint
- Touch up any bare spots from cutting

**Step 25 — Install ground surface:**
- Lay rubber playground tiles or horse stall mats under drop zones
- Spread 3–4 inches of mulch/rubber mulch in high-traffic areas
- Compact surface for even footing

## 7. SAFETY CHECKLIST

- [ ] Underground utilities marked (811 call)
- [ ] All concrete fully cured 72 hours before loading
- [ ] All lag bolts torqued and backed up with nuts (NO bolts without nuts)
- [ ] Pull-up bar tested with 300+ lb dynamic load
- [ ] Monkey bar rungs tested individually (150+ lb each)
- [ ] Posts plumb on two sides — verified with level
- [ ] Frame diagonals equal (square)
- [ ] Safety netting securely attached at all points — no gaps wider than 2"
- [ ] Shade sails tensioned properly — no sag that could trap water
- [ ] All swings have 6+ ft clearance from other equipment
- [ ] Impact-absorbing ground surface under all drop zones
- [ ] Overhead clearance of 15+ feet (no trees, wires, or structures)
- [ ] All hardware is galvanized or stainless (NO regular steel)
- [ ] All cut ends of lumber sealed with preservative

## 9. BUDGET OPTIMIZATION TIPS

- **Lumber yard vs. Home Depot:** Call local lumber yards — they often price 6×6s at $35–45 each vs. HD's $52–75. You could save $200+ on timber alone.
- **Buy surplus lumber:** Home Depot's "surplus lumber" section often has 6×6s at 50% off
- **Used equipment:** Facebook Marketplace for ropes, rings, swings, rubber mats
- **Galvanized hardware:** Buy in bulk from online (e.g., Fastenal or Grainger) vs. retail
- **Reclaimed wood:** For decking, bracing, and non-critical pieces
- **DIY monkey bar rungs:** Buy galvanized steel conduit (¾" EMT) from HD at ~$8/10 ft and cut to length — cheaper than pre-made rung sets
- **Netting substitute:** Construction safety netting from HD can work as a cheaper alternative to playground netting
