Session A — Flow + Mobility
3 rounds • 60 sec rest between rounds • 20–25 min total
LEOMOVES FUNDAMENTAL
Sidemonkey to Standup
Start in side monkey (one hand on floor, one leg wide). Explode up to standing using momentum.
6 reps/side. Focus on momentum, not raw strength.
LOCOMOTION
Shuffle Step
Get into a low squat, hands near floor. Shuffle side to side, staying low.
10 steps per direction. Hip rotation + coordination.
LOCOMOTION
Swipe Step
Cross one leg in front while stepping to the side, then straighten. Alternating sweep.
8 reps/side. Posterior chain + balance.
LOCOMOTION
Bear Crawl
All fours, knees hovering 1 inch off ground. Crawl opposite hand+foot together.
20 sec forward + 20 sec back.
LOCOMOTION
Crab Walk
Video not downloaded yet
Sit with hands behind, hips lifted. Walk forward with opposite hand+foot reach.
20 sec forward + 20 sec back. Opens hips AND shoulders.
